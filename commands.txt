Optional flags

--freeze to freeze the DenseNet base (feature extractor mode).

--lr 1e-4 to tweak the learning rate.

--seed 42 for reproducibility.

What it produces

Inside your --out folder:

training_curves.png — accuracy & loss curves

val_confusion_matrix.png — confusion matrix (validation)

val_acc_vs_precision.png — bar chart (val accuracy vs precision)

val_classification_report.txt — per-class precision/recall/F1

test_scores.txt — test accuracy & macro precision

best_densenet121.keras — best checkpoint

final_densenet121.keras — final saved model

label_to_index.json — class-name ↔ index mapping

demo_predictions.png — grid of random test images with predicted label & confidence
